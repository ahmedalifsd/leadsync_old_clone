# LeadSync Demo Sequence Playbook

Yeh document aapko complete demo flow deta hai, taake aap har prospect ko structured tareeqe se demo de sakain.

Audience: Business Owners + Their Team Leads/Employees

Goal: Prospect ko clear samajh aaye ke LeadSync unke business ke liye practical value kaise create karta hai.

---

## 1) Demo Se Pehle Preparation Checklist

Demo start karne se pehle yeh setup ready hona chahiye:

- Stable internet + backup hotspot
- 2 demo accounts:
  - Owner account
  - Employee account
- Pre-loaded demo data:
  - 20 to 50 leads (different statuses)
  - 2 to 3 team members
  - 1 role with limited permissions
  - 1 active plan + sample payment history
- Browser tabs prepared:
  - Home page
  - Owner dashboard login
  - Employee dashboard login
- Demo objective line ready:
  - "Aaj ka demo aapko dikhayega ke lead miss hone ka issue kaise khatam hota hai, team accountability kaise improve hoti hai, aur conversion visibility kaise milti hai."

---

## 2) Discovery Questions (Start Mein Yeh Zaroor Puchain)

Demo shuru karne se pehle 3 to 5 min discovery:

- Aap ka business model kya hai? (services, agency, sales team, etc.)
- Monthly approx kitni leads aati hain?
- Leads abhi kis system mein manage hoti hain? (Excel, WhatsApp, notebook)
- Sab se bara pain point kya hai?
  - missed follow-up
  - team tracking
  - low conversion
  - reporting issue
- Team size kya hai? aur kitne log lead handle karte hain?

Transition line:
"Perfect, ab main demo exactly aap ke use-case ke hisaab se dikhata hoon."

---

## 3) Demo Structure (High-Level Sequence)

Recommended 35 to 45 min flow:

1. Problem framing + expected outcomes (2 min)
2. Public value proposition (home page) (3 min)
3. Owner workflow demo end-to-end (20 min)
4. Employee workflow demo (10 min)
5. Reporting + ROI recap (5 min)
6. Q&A + close (5 min)

## 7) Objection Handling (Ready Responses)

### Objection 1: "Hum to Excel use karte hain"
Response:
"Excel storage ke liye theek hai, lekin live assignment, activity logs, role permissions, aur conversion visibility ke liye operationally weak hota hai. LeadSync Excel ke comparison mein execution layer deta hai."

### Objection 2: "Team adopt karegi ya nahi?"
Response:
"Employee interface simple aur task-focused hota hai. Hum phased onboarding suggest karte hain: pehle assigned leads + status updates, phir advanced features."

### Objection 3: "ROI kaise measure hoga?"
Response:
"3 KPIs track karein: response time, follow-up completion rate, conversion ratio. Usually in 2 to 4 weeks trend improvement visible hota hai."

### Objection 4: "Data security?"
Response:
"Role-based permissions aur controlled access model se sensitive workflows protected rehte hain."
